How Ensemble-Based Allocation Works
The Stacking Ensemble assigns each district an Ensemble Priority Score, a weighted combination of predicted risk tier and model confidence. Total doses are distributed according to this score, with Tier 1 districts always receiving full demand first. Model disagreement flags a district for field-level clinical review before dispatch.
Step 1, Tier Prediction
Three base learners each predict tier 1 to 4. Meta-learner combines via optimised weights.
Step 2, Priority Score
Formula combines tier and confidence. Disagreement applies a 15% penalty until reviewed.
Step 3, Dose Assignment
Doses split proportionally to priority. Tier 1 satisfied first, with a 20% equity floor.
